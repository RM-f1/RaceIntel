# Findings

## Race Results

This analysis uses the 2024 British Grand Prix race session as the primary case study.

Race results were analyzed to examine:

- Grid positions
- Finishing positions
- Position changes
- Championship points
- Driver classification status
---

## Position Changes

Several drivers gained positions relative to their starting grid, while others lost positions due to race pace, strategy, or incidents.

---

## Lap Analysis

Lap times generally increased over longer stints due to tyre degradation and race conditions.

Pit laps showed significantly higher lap times and were excluded from pace analysis.

---

## Tyre Strategy

Drivers completed multiple stints using different tyre compounds.

Average lap pace varied across compounds.

---

## Telemetry

Fastest-lap telemetry showed measurable differences in:

- Speed profiles
- Throttle application
- Brake usage

These observations describe driving characteristics without assigning qualitative judgments.

---

## Weather

Weather remained relatively stable during the race.

No rainfall was recorded.

Track conditions provide useful context for interpreting performance.
